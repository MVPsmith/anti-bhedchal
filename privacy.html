Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy covers the Anti Bhedchal channel and "Anti Bhedchal Analytics", a private tool the channel owner uses to read the channel's own YouTube statistics.

What the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the tool reads YouTube Analytics and YouTube Data for the Anti Bhedchal channel only, using read-only access (yt-analytics.readonly and youtube.readonly). It reads figures such as views, watch time, traffic sources and video titles. It cannot upload, edit or delete anything.

How the data is used and stored

The figures are stored on a private server controlled by the channel owner and used only to understand which videos work. They are never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The tool does not collect data about viewers beyond the aggregated statistics YouTube provides to channel owners.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions.

Viewers of the channel

Watching our videos on YouTube or Instagram is governed by those platforms' own privacy policies. This site sets no cookies and runs no trackers.
